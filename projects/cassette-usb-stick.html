Cassette USB Stick

A 3D-printed USB 3.2 stick shaped like a cassette tape. The stick swings out of the shell on a pivot made from a length of 1.75 mm filament.

ForPersonal project
Size2.32 × 1.50 × 0.35 in
MaterialPLA (ABS as an alternative)
Printed weightAbout 0.4 oz
StatusIn progress
The designIterationNextProof

The design

  • Parts: front and back shell, USB sled (tray and cover), reels, window, pivot pin, five screws, the PCB and a Type-A plug.
  • Mechanism: a swivel joint, from 0° stowed to 90° deployed.

Iteration

The first version had the sled clashing with the shell between roughly 5° and 20° of swing. The sled's rear rims were cut to an arc around the pivot, giving 0.016 in of clearance. An interference check is now clean from 0° to 90° in 1° steps.

Next

  • Detents to hold it at 0° and 90°.
  • User parameters, so the clearances are no longer hard-coded.
