Pixel Platformer

A 2D platformer written in pure Java, with no game engine: three levels, pixel art and a runnable JAR.

ForPersonal project
StartedJuly 2026

What it isWhat went into itProof

What it is

A playable vertical slice: title screen, three levels, then a win or lose screen. Art is the free Kenney Pixel Platformer pack (CC0).

What went into it

  • Movement with coyote time, jump buffering and variable jump height.
  • Enemies you can stomp, coins, spikes and a level-end flag.
  • A smooth camera clamped to the level, a parallax background and crisp 3× pixel scaling.
  • Levels written as text files, with grass and dirt tiles blended automatically from their neighbours.
  • Packaged with Gradle as a single runnable JAR.
