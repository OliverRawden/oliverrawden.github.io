I'm Oliver Rawden, based in the UK. I build practical software tools and design
physical products for small businesses.

I work across code and physical design: Java, Swift and C++ on one side,
Fusion 360 and a 3D printer on the other. I like taking a problem from a rough
brief to a working thing, and I keep a record of how I got there: the sketches,
the failed versions and the fixes.

### What I can do for you

- **Custom software tools:** desktop apps, command-line tools and automations that remove a repetitive job.
- **Product design and prototyping:** from a brief to Fusion 360 CAD, mock-ups and 3D-printed prototypes.
- **AI agents and email automation:** set-ups that draft and sort routine work, with a person approving anything that matters. See [Email Autopilot](https://rawden.tech/).
