About

I'm Oliver Rawden, based in the UK. I work across code and physical design: Java, Swift and C++ on one side, Fusion 360 and a 3D printer on the other.

I like taking a problem from a rough brief to a working thing, and I keep a record of how I got there: the sketches, the failed versions and the fixes. Outside of work I row competitively.
